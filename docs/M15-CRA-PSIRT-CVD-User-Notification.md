# M15 — CRA PSIRT / Coordinated Vulnerability Disclosure / User Notification

M15 adds deterministic PSIRT intake/triage, coordinated-vulnerability-disclosure policy/contact evidence, third-party component-maintainer coordination preparation, fixed-vulnerability advisory readiness and structured user-notification evidence for AEV/severe-incident cases.

## Boundaries

M15 prepares signed evidence only. It does **not** send email, contact maintainers, publish vulnerability advisories, dispatch user notifications, submit ENISA reports or claim CRA conformity. External effects require an authorised human workflow. M16 owns secure-update delivery/lifecycle controls.

## CRA traceability

M15 targets Article 13(6), Article 13(17), Article 14(8), Annex I Part II(4)–(6), and Annex II(1)–(2), using the immutable M11 requirement matrix as the legal locator baseline.

## Evidence

Node1 signs the M15 evidence manifest. Node2 receives public-key/verifier material only and fails closed on tamper or private-key transfer.
